Summary

  • The App does not require you to create an account.
  • Your nickname, avatar, game progress, coins, items, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • The App uses third-party advertising and measurement services that may process device identifiers and app-usage information.
  • We do not receive your locally stored game progress or use your nickname for advertising.
  • You can limit tracking through your device settings.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally in its private application storage:

  • The nickname and avatar you select
  • Game progress, completed levels, scores, and statistics
  • In-game coins, tickets, power-ups, and reward history
  • Daily check-in and lucky-draw status
  • Language, audio, display, and other preferences
  • Ad frequency and reward-revival counters

This information is used to provide game functionality and save your progress. It is not uploaded to our servers. It may be included in device backups if you have enabled backups through Apple or another backup service.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information, such as your real full name, address, phone number, or financial information, as your nickname.

2. Information Processed by Third Parties

The App uses third-party services that may automatically collect information when you use the App.

Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob and related Google Mobile Ads services to display rewarded, interstitial, and app-open advertisements and to measure advertising performance.

Google may process:

  • IP address
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as IDFA or IDFV where permitted
  • Device model, operating system, language, and app version
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and interactions
  • Diagnostic, performance, and fraud-prevention information

This information may be used to deliver and personalize advertisements, limit ad frequency, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal requirements.

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before advertisements are loaded. On iOS, you can also control tracking through Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. Your Choices and Rights
